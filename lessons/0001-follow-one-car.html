Lesson 1: follow one car

Goal: trace one workload request to one browser animation.

1. Input

parallel_driver_add_car.py:11–16 asks the SDK to choose a road and sends POST /cars.

road = random_choice(["north", "east", "south", "west"])

2. Queue

road_api.py:53–55 calls Road.enqueue_car(). road.py:112–127 accepts the car unless the queue already has 20.

3. Phase

The leader reaches road.py:188–207 only after the old phase ends and all four roads report all-red. scheduler.py:11–21 chooses the road. The leader appends that choice to the consensus log.

4. Commit

consensus.py:273–287 counts replicas. Three copies form a majority. apply_committed() passes the phase into Road.apply_command().

5. Movement

road.py:140–161 derives the light. During green, road.py:163–186 removes one queued car and emits movement_started.

6. Check and display

visualizer.py:113–146 checks permission and overlap. It sends the movement over SSE. public/app.js draws the car.

Quick check

  1. Does the workload choose a phase? No. It only creates demand.
  2. What makes a phase authoritative? A majority commit.
  3. Does the SVG decide collision safety? No. Structured movement times do.
